What makes 65% gross margin possible65% 毛利从哪来

  • Own green bean, own roast.自有豆源、自有烘焙。 The largest cost line is internalised, so margin is designed rather than negotiated.最大的一项成本被内部化，毛利是设计出来的，不是谈出来的。
  • Aeroponic yield stability.水培产量稳定。 No crop-year swings, no spot-price exposure on the core blend.不受产季波动影响，核心拼配不暴露在现货价格上。
  • Menu engineered for margin.菜单为毛利设计。 The recommended cup is also the highest-margin cup.最被推荐的杯子，就是毛利最高的杯子。
  • No rent, only pitch fees.无租金，只有点位费。 固定成本 stays controllable when a pitch underperforms.点位表现差时，固定成本是可调的。

A cart that pays back in 10 months compounds into 20 carts.10 个月回本的车，会自己滚成 20 辆。

The unit economics are the expansion plan. Everything else — flagship, pod, roastery — is financed by this number working. 单元经济本身就是扩张计划。旗舰店、种植舱、烘焙中心，全靠这个数字成立才有钱做。
