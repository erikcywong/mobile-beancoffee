The store must exist — but it must not be rolled out first. 固定店必须有，但不能先铺。

Its role is not acquisition volume. It is the brand flagship, the aeroponic experience hall, the cupping lab, the member centre and the regional training station. One per region, never a street-front rollout. 它的角色不是获客主力，而是品牌旗舰 + 水培体验馆 + 杯测实验室 + 会员中心 + 区域培训站。它不追求数量，不追求街铺密度，只做品牌锚点和区域中枢。

Three fatal weaknesses of a cart-only network纯移动车的三个死穴

!

Weak brand trust品牌信任弱

Specialty coffee sells professionalism and consistent experience. A cart proves presence, not permanence — and premium pricing needs permanence.精品咖啡需要专业感和稳定体验。移动车只能证明「在」，不能证明「稳」；而溢价恰恰需要稳定感。

⚑

Policy, weather, pitch risk政策、天气、点位风险

Licences, rain, venue disputes and lost pitches can remove revenue overnight. No single cart is a dependable asset.证照、雨天、场地纠纷、点位丢失，收入可以一夜归零。单辆车不是可靠的资产。

▤

Five things a cart cannot host车里做不了的五件事

Cupping sessions, member events, bean retail, B2B negotiations, franchisee training.杯测、会员活动、豆子零售、B2B 洽谈、加盟商培训。

So the store exists — but it is built to answer these five jobs, not to chase footfall. 所以固定店必须有，但它是为这五件事而建，不是为追客流而建。

Events, subscriptions, stored value — the retention engine that the carts' customer list feeds into.活动、订阅、储值 —— 承接移动车沉淀的会员名单，做留存转化。

Three things the flagship never becomes旗舰店永远不会变成的三件事

  • Not a street-front network.不铺街铺。 No density targets, no A/B/C site tiers — one anchor per region.不设密度指标，不做 A/B/C 类店分级，每区域一个锚点。
  • Not a normal cafe.不是普通咖啡馆。 If the menu could belong to any chain, the store has lost its reason to exist.如果菜单可以属于任何连锁品牌，这家店就失去了存在理由。
  • Not open to small franchisees.不开放小加盟。 Held by HQ or a regional partner — the store carries brand risk, not a single operator's.由总部或区域合伙人持有，品牌风险不由单个小经营者承担。
