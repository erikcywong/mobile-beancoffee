Structure · Control points结构 · 控制点

Franchisees invest in carts. HQ owns the bean, the roast, the app and the brand. 加盟商只投移动车。总部控豆、控烘焙、控小程序、控品牌。

Capital is split by risk appetite, not by territory. Light units go to franchisees, heavy assets go to regional partners, and the supply chain stays with HQ — which is where the profit is designed to sit. 资金按风险承受力切分，而不按地域切分。轻单元给加盟商，重资产给区域合伙人，供应链留在总部 —— 利润被刻意设计在这一层。

Note what is missing: no party other than HQ can buy the bean elsewhere, and no franchisee owns a store. That single rule keeps the network from fragmenting into independent cafes wearing the same colours. 注意缺席的两件事：除总部外没有任何一方能另找豆源，也没有任何加盟商持有固定店。这一条规则让网络不会碎成一批穿同一件外衣的独立咖啡馆。

Control points控制点

Four things HQ never outsources总部永不放手的四件事

◆

The bean控豆

Aeroponic lots, SCA 85+, single supply origin. Quality and cost are set upstream of every cart.水培批次、SCA 85+、唯一供应来源。品质与成本在每一辆车之前就被定死。

▤

The roast控烘焙

One roast profile per SKU, produced in HQ roasteries. No local roasting, ever.每个 SKU 只有一个烘焙曲线，全部在总部烘焙中心完成，不允许本地自烘。

▢

The mini-programme控小程序

Orders, stored value and the member list belong to the platform, not the operator.点单、储值、会员名单属于平台，不属于门店经营者。

⚑

The brand控品牌

Pricing, menu, visual identity, certification claims. Standard or gone.定价、菜单、视觉、认证话术。达不到标准就不能挂这个牌子。

Who this unit suits这个单元适合谁

  • Can run a cart 6 days a week themselves, or with one partner能自己或和 1 名伙伴一周跑 6 天
  • Has ¥150k–250k of own capital, understands a 10-month payback自有资金 15—25 万，能接受 10 个月左右的回本周期
  • Comfortable being told where to park and what to charge愿意接受总部指定的点位与定价
  • Wants a second and third unit within 18 months目标 18 个月内上第二、第三辆
  • Not for investors who want a passive store-front asset不适合想要被动持有店面的投资人
Support system支持体系

What HQ delivers to every cart总部给每一辆车交付什么

Pitch library & rotation plan点位库与轮转计划

Validated pitches, day-rate terms, and the schedule that moves carts to where they perform.已验证点位、日结条款，以及把车调到高效位置的排班。

Standardised vehicle and fit-out标准化车辆与改装

One spec, one maintenance network, one spare-parts catalogue.一套规格、一个维护网络、一份备件清单。

Barista certification at the regional store区域店完成咖啡师认证

Cupping table, extraction dial-in, service script — pass or don't open.杯测台、萃取参数、服务话术 —— 不合格不开业。

Bean and consumable logistics豆子与耗材物流

Delivered from the city roastery to the hub, then to carts on a fixed cadence.从城市烘焙中心到区域中枢，再按固定节奏配到车上。

Platform and member data平台与会员数据

Operators see their own dashboard; HQ sees the network. No data silos.经营者看自己的看板，总部看全网。不留数据孤岛。

Quality audit and mystery visits品控巡查与暗访

Because a bad cup anywhere is a bad cup everywhere.因为任何一杯做坏，都是全网做坏。

Profit split principle利润分配原则

  • Franchisee加盟商 — keeps cart gross margin; earns the operational effort拿车端毛利，赚运营的钱
  • Regional partner区域合伙人 — keeps store and hub profit; earns the regional build拿门店与中枢利润，赚区域建设的钱
  • HQ总部 — earns the supply-chain spread; scales with throughput赚供应链差价，随吞吐量增长
