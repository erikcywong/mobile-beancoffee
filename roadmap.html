Sequence · Mobile first, flagship second先后顺序 · 移动车为主，固定店为辅

Prove acquisition with carts, then spend on brand. 先用移动车证明获客能力，再为品牌花钱。

Each phase has one job and one entry gate. Capital is only released to the next layer when the previous layer's numbers are printed. 每个阶段只干一件事，且只有一道准入闸口。上一层的数据没有印出来，就不给下一层放钱。

Phases阶段

The path, month by month逐月展开的路径

Phase 1 — Test the market with carts第一阶段 —— 用移动车测试市场

0–12 months个月 Acquisition + validation获客 + 验证
  • 3–5 direct carts3—5 辆直营移动车
  • Fitted with a small aeroponic demo module带小型雾化水培展示模块
  • Rotating through parks, markets, scenic areas, offices, university towns跑园区、市集、景区、写字楼、大学城
  • Mini-programme ordering to accumulate members小程序点单，沉淀会员
  • Third-party SCA 85+ certification and blind-test report displayed on board第三方 SCA 85+ 认证和盲测报告随车展示
  • Target: 80–120 cups/day目标：日均 80—120 杯
  • Gross margin 65%+毛利 65%+
  • Payback in 8–14 months回本 8—14 个月

Phase 2 — Turn data into one flagship第二阶段 —— 把数据变成一家旗舰店

12–24 months个月 Brand + retention品牌 + 留存
  • Pick one high-repeat district using cart data用移动车数据选出一个高复购区域
  • Open 1 flagship experience store, 80–120 sqm开 1 家旗舰体验店，80—120 平
  • Not a normal cafe: aeroponic experience + cupping + bean retail + franchise training不做普通咖啡馆，做「水培咖啡体验 + 杯测 + 豆子零售 + 加盟培训」
  • Fleet expands to 8–10 carts with the first franchise units车队扩至 8—10 辆，出现首批加盟车
  • Store held by HQ or a regional partner固定店由总部或区域合伙人持有
  • No small-franchise ownership of the store不开放小加盟持有固定店

Phase 3 — Build the granary behind the network第三阶段 —— 在网络背后建粮仓

24+ months个月 Scale + supply chain规模 + 供应链
  • One grow pod and one roastery per city一个城市配 1 个种植舱 + 烘焙中心
  • 10–20 mobile carts per city单城 10—20 辆移动车
  • Franchisees invest only in carts加盟商只投移动车
  • Regional partners invest in flagship stores and grow pods区域合伙人投旗舰店和种植舱
  • HQ controls the bean, the roast, the mini-programme and the brand总部控豆、控烘焙、控小程序、控品牌
  • Profit comes from the supply chain, not franchise fees利润来自供应链，不是靠加盟费
  • Carts stay asset-light; the granary holds the margin车队保持轻资产，毛利留在粮仓

Gates闸口

What must be true before the next phase is funded进入下一阶段前必须成立的条件

No gate, no capital. These thresholds protect the store and the granary from being built on an unproven cart fleet.没有闸口就不放钱。这些门槛保证店铺和粮仓不会建在一支未经证明的车队之上。

Gate 1 → Phase 2闸口 1 → 第二阶段

  • At least 3 pitches stable for 6 consecutive months至少 3 个点位连续 6 个月稳定
  • Top pitches ≥100 cups/dayTop 点位日均 ≥100 杯
  • Repeat-purchase rate ≥30% in one district单一区域复购率 ≥30%
  • Member base ≥8,000 within the city同城会员数 ≥8,000
  • Payback ≤14 months on live units在运车辆回本 ≤14 个月

Gate 2 → Phase 3闸口 2 → 第三阶段

  • 10+ carts live in one city单城车队 ≥10 辆
  • Bean retail + membership ≥25% of flagship revenue豆子零售 + 会员占旗舰店收入 ≥25%
  • Franchisee average payback ≤12 months加盟商平均回本 ≤12 个月
  • Supply-chain gross margin covers HQ fixed cost供应链毛利可覆盖总部固定成本
  • Bean demand predictable within ±15%豆子需求量可预测在 ±15% 内

System-wide sales vs HQ supply-chain revenue系统终端销售额 vs 总部供应链收入

The gap between the two bars is the argument: HQ grows on throughput, not on entry fees. 两条柱之间的差距就是论点：总部靠吞吐量增长，而不是靠入场费。
