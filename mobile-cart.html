Rationale理由

Why the cart comes first为什么移动车优先

01

It finds people主动找人

No dependence on natural footfall. The cart goes to where demand is concentrated at that hour — office lunch, campus evening, weekend market.不依赖自然客流，去需求最集中的地方和时段：写字楼午高峰、大学城晚间、周末市集。

02

Cheap to be wrong试错成本极低

If a pitch doesn't work, move it next week. A store that fails locks in rent, renovation and six months of tokens.一个点位不行就换，下周就能重来。固定店选错，租金、装修和时间全部沉没。

03

Little rent pressure租金压力小

Pitch fees are day-rate or event based, not a five-year lease. Cash conversion is measured in weeks, not years.点位费按日或按活动结算，不是五年租约。现金回收以周计，而不是以年计。

04

A light franchise unit加盟商投得进

¥150k–250k is a decision a working couple or a small operator can make. A fixed store requires capital and conviction nobody has yet.15—25 万是一个普通经营者能做出的决定；固定店需要的资金和信心，现阶段没人愿意先给。

05

Data decides real estate数据决定未来选址

Repeat-purchase data from carts tells you which district deserves a flagship. Site selection becomes an output, not a bet.跑出来的复购数据直接决定未来固定店开在哪 —— 选址变成推论结果，而不是赌注。

→

Therefore所以

The cart is the unit of expansion. Everything downstream — store, pod, roastery — is justified by what the fleet proves in the first 12 months.移动车就是扩张单元。后面的店、种植舱、烘焙中心，都由车队前 12 个月跑出的数据来背书。
