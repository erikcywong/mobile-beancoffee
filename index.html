Programmable yield产量可编程

Aeroponic cultivation decouples output from season, altitude and rainfall. Supply becomes a planning variable.水培把产量从季节、海拔与降雨中解耦，供给变成可规划的变量。

Controllable cost成本可控

Cost per cup is locked at the front end, so a mobile unit can be priced for volume distribution rather than scarcity.单杯成本在前端锁定，移动单元可以按流量定价，而不是按稀缺定价。

The cart is not a downmarket substitute. It is the distribution layer that de-risks everything built on top of it. 移动车不是低端替代，而是让上层资产不冒风险的分销层。

Mobile carts are the spear, stores are the flag, grow pods and roasteries are the granary.
移动车是矛，固定店是旗，种植舱和烘焙中心是粮仓。

Acquire and validate with carts first; build brand and retention with a flagship second. Not either/or — mobile-first, fixed-store-second, in that order. 我会先用移动车获客和验证，再用固定旗舰店做品牌和留存。不是二选一，而是移动车为主、固定店为辅，先后分明。
